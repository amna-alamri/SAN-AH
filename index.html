Teen and Athar | An Omani Craftsman's Journey

In the historic lanes of Bahla—Oman's iconic heartland of traditional pottery—Salem Al-Busaidi sits at his water-powered potter’s wheel inside his family workshop. Having learned the art of pottery from his father at the age of ten, Salem grew up immersed in a craft that has spanned centuries of Omani heritage. What inspires Salem's creations is the majestic landscape and architectural spirit of Bahla itself.

He draws inspiration from the intricate geometric motifs carved into ancient fortresses and the warm, earthy hues of the local terrain. Through every piece he molds, Salem aims to preserve authentic Omani pottery while infusing a sleek, modern touch that complements contemporary homes. The creation process for each piece begins with harvesting and carefully purifying local clay from the wadis of Bahla, followed by hand-kneading to achieve a flawless texture. Using steady hands on the wheel, Salem shapes pitchers, cups, and traditional Jahla water jars one by one. The pieces are left to dry naturally in the shade before Salem hand-engraves unique traditional patterns using simple carving tools.

Finally, they are fired in high-temperature kilns to ensure durability and strength. Through SAN'AH, Salem shares a piece of Oman's soul and rich Bahla craftsmanship with every home.
